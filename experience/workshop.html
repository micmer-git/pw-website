Key Topics Covered

Six focused talks by the Particleworks Europe engineering team. Tap any topic to read more.

TOPIC 01
Opening topic

AI-Driven Particleworks: Automating and Optimizing Simulation Processes

Michele Merelli
Particleworks Europe · EnginSoft SpA · 🇮🇹 Italy
AI & automation Python APIDoEOptimization

Simulation is moving from a single-run activity to an automated, data-generating process. This talk shows how AI and scripting turn Particleworks into an engine that runs, monitors and post-processes itself — from parametric setup and design-of-experiments to automatic reporting.

We look at where AI genuinely adds value in a meshless CFD workflow: driving parametric studies, accelerating the exploration of the design space, extracting the right quantities from large result sets, and connecting simulation output to the engineering decisions that follow.

Speaker: Michele Merelli — Particleworks Europe / EnginSoft SpA, IT
TOPIC 02

Conjugate Heat Transfer: What's New in Version 9.0 and Applications

Riccardo Sala
Particleworks Europe · EnginSoft SpA · 🇮🇹 Italy
Thermal management CHTSolid conductionHTC mapping

Conjugate heat transfer couples the thermal behaviour of the fluid with conduction inside the solid parts it touches — the key ingredient when cooling performance, not just flow distribution, is what has to be predicted.

This talk covers what version 9.0 brings to CHT modelling, how to set it up correctly, and where it pays off: e-motor oil cooling, piston and gearbox thermal management, machine-tool cooling and other applications where local temperature and heat transfer coefficients drive the design.

Speaker: Riccardo Sala — Particleworks Europe / EnginSoft SpA, IT
TOPIC 03

Multi-Phase Flow: Methods for Air and Liquid Flow, with Use Cases

Gianluca Parma
Particleworks Europe · EnginSoft SpA · 🇮🇹 Italy
Multi-phase Air entrainmentTwo-phase MPSAeration

Many industrial flows are not liquid-only: air is entrained, trapped, compressed and released, and it changes what the liquid does. This talk reviews the modelling options available in Particleworks for air and liquid together — from lightweight approaches that account for the gas phase implicitly to fully resolved two-phase simulations.

Each method is placed against the question it answers, with use cases showing when the extra cost of resolving the air phase is repaid by the accuracy gained: aeration in gearboxes, filling and venting, sloshing with trapped air, and free-surface flows with strong gas–liquid interaction.

Speaker: Gianluca Parma — Particleworks Europe / EnginSoft SpA, IT
TOPIC 04

Fluid–Structure Interaction: Modeling Deformable Bodies Under Fluid Action

FG
Federica Galbiati
Particleworks Europe · EnginSoft SpA · 🇮🇹 Italy
FSI Deformable bodiesCoupled solversMultibody

When a component is flexible, the flow shapes the structure and the deformed structure changes the flow. This talk presents how Particleworks models deformable bodies under fluid action, and what the coupling strategy means in practice for set-up, stability and computational cost.

Typical applications include flexible films and packaging, seals and membranes, wipers and blades, and compliant components in fluid machinery — cases where a rigid-wall assumption would miss the physics that matters.

Speaker: Federica Galbiati — Particleworks Europe / EnginSoft SpA, IT
TOPIC 05

CFD + DEM Simulations: Coupling Fluid Flow with Granular Media

Riccardo Sala
Particleworks Europe · EnginSoft SpA · 🇮🇹 Italy
CFD-DEM GranuleworksParticle–fluid couplingWear

Coupling Particleworks with Granuleworks brings solid particles into the flow: powders, granules, abrasive media and debris that are transported, separated or that impact surfaces. This talk explains how the coupling works and which parameters govern the exchange between the fluid and the granular phase.

Applications span shot peening and surface treatment, separation and classification units, sediment and sand handling, mixing and coating of powders — with a look at how wear and coverage predictions are extracted from the coupled results.

Speaker: Riccardo Sala — Particleworks Europe / EnginSoft SpA, IT
TOPIC 06

Multi-Level Resolution and Particle Refinement Methods

FG
Federica Galbiati
Particleworks Europe · EnginSoft SpA · 🇮🇹 Italy
Accuracy vs. cost Multi-resolutionLocal refinementGPU efficiency

Particle size drives both accuracy and run time. Multi-level resolution lets you spend particles where the physics happens — thin films, jets, gaps and impingement zones — and coarsen everywhere else, without giving up the free-surface detail the method is chosen for.

This talk covers the refinement strategies available in Particleworks, how to choose and place the refinement regions, the practical rules for keeping the solution stable across resolution levels, and the speed-up that can realistically be expected on GPU.

Speaker: Federica Galbiati — Particleworks Europe / EnginSoft SpA, IT
