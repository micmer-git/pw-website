## 🎯 SHOT PEENING SIMULATION

Alpcan Güray from MTU Aero Engines AG will show how Particleworks models the shot peening of fatigue-critical aerospace components.

Leveraging coupled CFD-DEM simulation, the team was able to:
- Accurately predict shot trajectories from machine, nozzle and air-pressure parameters
- Estimate surface coverage and the resulting residual-stress distribution
- Optimise robotic path planning for homogeneous peening

## 💧 PELTON TURBINE EROSION

Jean Decaix from HES-SO Valais//Wallis will present Moving Particle Simulation of eroded Pelton runners.

- Simulate real erosion geometries from new, mid-life and end-of-life runners
