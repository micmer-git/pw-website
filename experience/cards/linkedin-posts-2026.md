## 🎯 SHOT PEENING SIMULATION

Alpcan Güray from MTU Aero Engines AG will show how Particleworks models the shot peening of fatigue-critical aerospace components.

Leveraging coupled CFD-DEM simulation, the team was able to:
- Accurately predict shot trajectories from machine, nozzle and air-pressure parameters
- Estimate surface coverage and the resulting residual-stress distribution
- Optimise robotic path planning for homogeneous peening

## 🌡️ RECIPROCATING PUMP THERMAL ANALYSIS

Leonardo Lanciotti from R&D CFD will show a conjugate-heat-transfer analysis of a reciprocating pump.

## 💧 PELTON TURBINE EROSION

Jean Decaix from HES-SO Valais//Wallis will present Moving Particle Simulation of eroded Pelton runners.

Leveraging GPU-accelerated MPS, the study was able to:
- Simulate real erosion geometries from new, mid-life and end-of-life runners
- Quantify torque losses of 6% and 6.6% for worn runners
- Capture complex free-surface, high-velocity flows on rotating buckets

## 🫧 AIR BUBBLE DYNAMICS IN E-DRIVETRAINS

René Kockisch from IAV GmbH will show air-bubble dynamics in the gear oil of an electric 3-speed commercial-vehicle drivetrain.

Leveraging the IAV Particle Explorer and high-speed imaging, the team was able to:
- Quantify bubble formation, transport, coalescence and dissolution
- Reveal characteristic bubble size distributions and preferred transport paths
- Support optimisation of transmission design and drivetrain efficiency
