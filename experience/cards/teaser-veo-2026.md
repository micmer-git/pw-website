# Particleworks Experience 2026 — 30s Teaser (Google Veo shot list)

A 30-second cinematic teaser, built as **10 × 3s beats** so each can be generated as
its own Veo clip and edited together. Vertical **9:16** is the primary deliverable for
LinkedIn/Instagram; a **16:9** master is noted at the end.

Brand look (keep identical across every clip):
- Palette: deep crimson→oxblood gradient background (#7E1416 → #3A0C0E), warm coral
  particles (#FFE2D4), single electric-green accent (#34D27C), pure-white type.
- Typography for on-screen text: heavy geometric sans (Officina Sans Bold / similar), tight tracking, uppercase for headlines.
- Mood: premium engineering, meshless-CFD "fluid made of light", Italian design restraint.
- Format: 24 fps, cinematic, shallow depth of field, fine volumetric haze, subtle film grain, high dynamic range.

## SHOT LIST (10 × 3s)

### 00:00–00:03 — Beat 1 · "Birth from particles"
- **Prompt:** A void of deep crimson. Thousands of tiny coral particles drift in from darkness and
  begin to swirl into a slow vortex, faint green sparks igniting among them. Pure abstract fluid-of-light.
- **Camera:** Slow dolly-in along the vortex axis, almost imperceptible push.
- **Lens / DoF:** 35mm, T1.8, shallow — foreground particles bokeh, core in focus.
- **Light:** Single warm key from frame-right, volumetric god-rays, deep falloff to black edges.
- **Motion:** Particles accelerate gently toward center; time feels suspended.
- **Text:** none.
- **SFX:** low sub-bass swell + airy particle shimmer rising.

### 00:03–00:06 — Beat 2 · "Fluid becomes form"
- **Prompt:** The swirling coral particles coalesce and resolve into the glistening surface of oil
  splashing over a rotating metal gear/e-motor rotor, droplets flinging outward in slow motion.
- **Camera:** 90° orbit around the rotor, smooth on a virtual gimbal.
- **Lens / DoF:** 50mm macro, T2.0, razor-thin focus sliding across the wet metal.
- **Light:** Hard rim light catching droplet edges, green bounce in the shadows.
- **Motion:** Slow-motion oil sheet, individual particles still visible in the splash (MPS look).
- **Text:** none.
- **SFX:** liquid whoosh + metallic resonance.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Generate per-beat (3s each)** for directorial control, same seed + the global style block, then
  cut together in any NLE. Veo audio can be muted in favor of one music bed.
- **Brand continuity:** drop `card-00-program.png` (and the crimson background) in as a reference image
  so every clip shares the exact background tone and coral particle color.
- **Text is safer in post.** Let Veo render clean visuals; add the headlines/CTA as crisp typographic
  overlays in edit (Officina Sans Bold, white + green underline) to avoid AI text artifacts.
- **Music:** one minimal driving electronic pulse, ~120 bpm, soft sub-bass; build from Beat 3, peak at
  Beat 9, resolve on Beat 10. Keep SFX low under the music.
- **Aspect variants:** primary **9:16** (social). For a **16:9** master, widen Beats 3/8/10 framing and
  keep titles centered; re-render those three rather than cropping.
- **Pacing:** Beats 1–3 are slow/hero (≈ one idea each); Beats 4–7 are a punchy montage (hard cuts on
  the beat); Beats 8–10 land the place, the people, the call to action.
