# Particleworks Experience 2026 — 10s Stop-Motion Teaser

A **real, hand-shot stop-motion** teaser (no AI). The whole idea is a visual pun: Particleworks
simulates fluids as **particles**, so we animate **real particles** that flow, swirl and re-form into
shapes — ending on the **Modena skyline** and a **tortellino** 🥟.

- **Length:** 10 s · **5 beats × 2 s**
- **Capture:** shoot **on twos at 12 fps → 120 frames total** (24 frames per beat). 15 fps (150
  frames) if you want it silkier.
- **Aspect:** **1:1** primary (LinkedIn feed) — overhead works for 9:16 too, just frame taller.

---

## THE LOOK
- Surface: matte dark-crimson card / oxblood felt (matches the card background).
- Particles: **coral/red** as the hero (brand), a touch of **electric-green** accent, **white/cream**
  for the food/Modena beats.
- One clean light direction, soft shadow, premium and minimal. Same palette as `card-00-program.png`.

## RIG & SETUP (top-down)
- **Camera locked overhead** on a copy-stand / tripod with horizontal arm, lens pointing straight down
  at the table. Manual focus, **manual exposure, manual white balance** (lock everything — no auto).
- Light: one softbox/LED at ~30° from the side for a gentle directional shadow, plus a white bounce
  card opposite. Kill all flicker (no window light).
- **Software:** Dragonframe (or Stop Motion Studio). Use **onion-skinning** to see the previous frame
  and move particles a hair at a time.
- Tools: tweezers, a soft brush, a stiff card/scraper, and **laser-cut or printed stencils** of each
  target shape placed *under* a glass sheet as a guide (remove in post / shoot just inside the lines).

## MATERIALS (the "particles")
- Hero coral/red: red split lentils, red aquarium sand, paprika, or red nonpareils (sprinkles).
- Green accent: green lentils / pistachio crumb / green sand.
- White/cream + the food beats: **semolina & "00" flour** (it IS tortellino dough), couscous, sugar.
- Modena beat: a **laser-cut Ghirlandina + Duomo silhouette stencil**.
- Final beat: **one real fresh tortellino** (or a small one shaped from pasta dough on set).
- Optional: a thin magnetic tray + iron filings under a magnet for a cool "particles align to field" move.

## SHOT LIST (5 × 2 s · 24 frames each)

### Beat 1 · 0–2 s — "Scatter to swirl"  *(brand reveal)*
- **Action:** Start with a tight pile of coral particles dead-center. Frame by frame, nudge them
  outward into a slow **spiral/vortex** — the meshless-CFD swirl.
- **How:** Work from the finished spiral *backwards* (remove/contract toward center), then **play the
  clip reversed** so it looks like it self-assembles. Use the brush for smooth arcs.
- **Text:** none.
- **SFX:** soft granular "shhhh" + low sub rise.

### Beat 2 · 2–4 s — "The mark"  *(logo / title)*
- **Action:** The swirl collapses inward and the particles settle into the **Particleworks pictogram**
  (the rounded particle cluster) in coral + green, with a small printed lockup **PARTICLEWORKS
  EXPERIENCE 2026** sliding in beside it.
- **How:** Stencil the pictogram outline under glass; build it by adding particles a few per frame.
  Keep the green dots deliberate (place with tweezers).
- **Text:** **PARTICLEWORKS EXPERIENCE 2026** (cleaner added as a typographic overlay in edit).
- **SFX:** soft "settle" thump; music pulse enters (~120 bpm, minimal).

### Beat 3 · 4–6 s — "It simulates this"  *(engineering wink)*
- **Action:** The mark bursts apart and the particles **re-form into a gear / oil-droplet outline**
  (nod to gearboxes, e-motor cooling, lubrication) — then a single green droplet "falls" across frame.
- **How:** Second stencil (gear or droplet). Same build/reverse trick. The falling droplet = a green
  cluster moved a few mm per frame in an arc.
- **Text (small lower strip):** **MESHLESS CFD · 10 INDUSTRIAL & ACADEMIC TALKS**.
- **SFX:** mechanical tick + a light "blip" on the droplet.

### Beat 4 · 6–8 s — "Modena"  *(the skyline)*
- **Action:** Particles sweep left→right and **assemble the Modena skyline silhouette** — the
  **Ghirlandina tower** and the Duomo roofline — in coral, with white/cream particles as a soft "sky".
- **How:** Ghirlandina + Duomo **stencil** under glass; build the silhouette bottom-up. Sweep motion =
  translate the whole field a few mm per frame while the skyline "grows."
- **Text:** **MODENA, ITALY**.
- **SFX:** warm ambient swell, music lifts.

### Beat 5 · 8–10 s — "Tortellino + invite"  *(the wink + CTA)*
- **Action:** From the skyline, a clump of cream/flour particles rolls into the foreground and **curls
  into a single golden tortellino** (or a real tortellino is revealed sitting in the particles). A
  green accent line draws under it; the end card lands.
- **How:** Animate flour gathering into a ring, then **swap in the real tortellino** over 2–3 frames
  for the reveal (classic substitution). Dust a little semolina around it for life.
- **Text (end card):** **PARTICLEWORKS EXPERIENCE 2026** / **6–7 OCTOBER · MODENA** /
  **REGISTER FREE — particleworks-europe.com/experience**.
- **SFX:** playful final "pop"/wink chime; music resolves clean.

## TECHNIQUE NOTES
- **Shoot morphs in reverse.** It's far easier to *deconstruct* a finished shape (sweep particles away)
  than to build a clean one blind. Capture the teardown, reverse it in edit → flawless "assembly."
- **Onion-skin every frame**; move particles only 1–3 mm at a time for smooth flow. Resist big jumps.
- **Lock the table.** Tape stencils, mark camera + light positions; if the rig shifts, the morph breaks.
- **Twos, not ones.** 12 fps on twos gives the tactile stop-motion charm and halves your shooting (120
  frames for 10 s). Go to ones only for the fastest moves if they strobe.
- **Text & CTA in post.** Shoot clean particle visuals; add the wordmark, lower-thirds and CTA as crisp
  type in your editor (heavy geometric sans, white + a green underline) so it stays sharp and on-brand.
- **Backup plan for "flow":** a magnetic tray + iron filings under a moving magnet gives an effortless
  "particles align to a field" motion for Beats 1/3 if hand-nudging is too fiddly.

## MUSIC / SOUND
- One minimal electronic bed, ~120 bpm, soft sub-bass; enter at Beat 2, peak at Beat 4, resolve on
  Beat 5. Layer the granular SFX low underneath.

## DELIVERABLES
