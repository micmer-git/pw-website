Engineering insights

Three numbers from the gearbox report

2.1×

ISO over-predicts the churning loss

118 W by ISO/TR 14179-2 against 55 W simulated. ISO has no term for gears fed by thrown oil, which is most of this box.

3–6×

Gears cool harder than the housing

Mean HTC 214 W/m²K on a gear against 36 on the housing. Relative velocity drives it, not how wet the surface is.

0

HTC in an adiabatic run

Zero non-zero values over 670,920 triangles. The thermal map needed a real re-run, not a post-process.

Demonstration gearbox, Particleworks 8.2, 375 k particles, 2 s run. No customer data.
