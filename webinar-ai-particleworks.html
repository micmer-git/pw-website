AI-accelerated Particleworks: the workshop, in 45 minutes

The key results from our Particleworks Experience 2026 workshop — AI and scripting, fluid–structure interaction, multi-phase flow, CFD + DEM, refinement and heat transfer — condensed into one live session.

  • Online

Agenda

Five focused topics and a short Q&A. Every topic comes from a talk of the workshop; simulations shown are real Particleworks runs.

  1. 15:0010 min

    01AI and scripting as a simulation engine

    How an AI coding agent drives Particleworks through its Python API: scene set-up, pre-processing, batch runs, monitoring and automatic reporting — and where AI genuinely adds value in a meshless CFD workflow.

    AI agentsPython APIDoE
    Scripted solver extension: hydration computed on every particle and fed back into the flow.
  2. 15:108 min

    02Fluid–structure interaction

    Deformable bodies under fluid action: how the pressure load is passed to a structural model and the deformed shape returned to the flow, step after step.

    Two-way couplingFlexible walls
    Flexible sheets deforming under impact — a parametric array run from one script.
  3. 15:188 min

    03Multi-phase flow: air and liquid

    Methods for air–liquid flows and free-surface problems, with practical use cases and the modelling choices that decide whether a result can be trusted.

    Air & liquidNon-NewtonianFree surface
    Two non-Newtonian fluids pouring and mixing on a shared surface.
  4. 15:267 min

    04CFD + DEM: fluid meets granular media

    Coupling fluid flow with particles and powders — where the two solvers exchange momentum and how to set up a coupled case without losing the physics.

    ParticleworksGranuleworksCoupling
  5. 15:337 min

    05Multi-level resolution and conjugate heat transfer

    Particle refinement where it matters, plus what's new for conjugate heat transfer in Particleworks 9.0 and the applications it opens.

    RefinementCHTVersion 9.0
